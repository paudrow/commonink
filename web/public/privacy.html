Common Ink

Privacy Policy

Effective October 17, 2026

The short version

  • We collect what it takes to run your notebook: your name, email and picture from Google, the notes and files you put in, and, if you subscribe, your billing status from Stripe.
  • If you connect Google Calendar, Contacts or Drive, we only use that data to show and sync it for you. We never use it for ads or to train AI models.
  • We don't sell your data, show ads, use tracking cookies, or train AI models on your content.
  • People you share a workspace with can see what's in it. Agents you connect can do what you can do.
  • You can ask us for a copy of your data, or to delete it, at any time.

Who we are

Common Ink (“we”, “us”) is a notebook for people, their teams and their AI agents, at commonink.app. It is operated by Audrow Nash LLC. Questions about this policy go to support@commonink.app.

What we collect

  • Your Google account basics. When you sign in with Google we receive your name, email address, profile picture and a Google account ID. Signing in doesn't give us access to anything else in your Google account.
  • Google data you choose to connect. Only if you connect them: your Google Calendar events, your Google Contacts, and the files Common Ink saves to your Google Drive. See Google user data below.
  • What you put in Common Ink. Notes, uploaded files, and the workspaces, members and invites you create.
  • History. Each change records who made it (you, a teammate or an agent), when, and the note's previous text, so changes can be seen and undone.
  • Billing. If billing applies to you, when your trial ends, and, if you subscribe, your Stripe customer and subscription IDs, your plan (monthly or yearly), its status and when it renews. Stripe collects your payment details directly; we never see or store your full card number.
  • Agents and apps you connect. Each connection's name, which workspaces it can use, and when it was made, so you can see and disconnect it.
  • Technical logs. Our hosting provider records requests (such as IP address, browser and the page requested) to keep the service running and secure. These logs are kept for up to 7 days.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and show you the workspaces you belong to.
  • To store, search, sync and display your notes and files, including to the people you share them with.
  • To keep the service secure, prevent abuse and fix problems.
  • To run your trial and subscription, and to know whether your workspaces should be editable.
  • To contact you about your account, your subscription or important changes to the service.

We don't sell or rent your personal information, use it for advertising, or use your notes and files to train AI models. Common Ink doesn't send your notes to an AI model itself; that only happens when you connect an agent and it reads them.

Google user data

Sign-in uses only your basic profile (name, email address, profile picture). Everything else below happens only if you choose to connect it, and Common Ink asks Google for each permission the first time you use the feature that needs it.

  • Google Calendar (calendar.readonly, and calendar.events if you turn on editing). We read your calendars and their events (titles, times, descriptions, locations, organizers and attendees) to show them in Common Ink's calendar and to make meeting notes from them when you ask. Events are copied into your workspace so the calendar works quickly, and only you see your Google calendars there, even in a team workspace. If you turn on editing, we change events only when you do: adding, moving or deleting an event you edit in Common Ink, and adding a link to its meeting note at the end of its description. Google sends no invitations for changes made here.
  • Google Contacts (contacts.readonly, and contacts if you allow editing). We read your contacts' names, email addresses, phone numbers, organizations, job titles, links and nicknames, and write them into the People notes of the workspace you sync from. If you sync into a team workspace, its members can see those notes, like any other note there. If you allow editing, changes you make to those fields in Common Ink are sent back to Google. The rest of a person's note is never sent to Google.
  • Google Drive (drive.file). When you save a note to Drive, we create it in a “Common Ink” folder in your Drive. This permission only lets us see files Common Ink created; we can't see the rest of your Drive.

We use Google user data only to provide these features to you. We don't sell it, use it for advertising, or use it to develop, improve or train AI or machine learning models, ours or anyone else's. We don't transfer it to anyone except as needed to provide these features (for example, to our hosting provider), to comply with the law, or as part of a merger or acquisition with your notice. No person at Common Ink reads it unless you ask us to (for example, for support), it's needed for security or to investigate abuse, or the law requires it.

Google access and refresh tokens are encrypted and are never sent to your browser. When you disconnect Google (in Settings, under Integrations) or delete your account, we revoke Common Ink's access with Google, delete the tokens, and remove your Google calendars and their copied events from every workspace. People notes made from your contacts stay in the workspace as ordinary notes, no longer synced, until you delete them. You can also remove Common Ink's access in your Google Account settings.

Common Ink's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Who can see your content

  • Your personal workspace is visible only to you.
  • Team workspaces are visible to every member. Anyone with a valid invite link (valid for 7 days) can join the team it was created for.
  • Notes and folders you share are visible to the people or email addresses you share them with, or, for a link, to anyone who has the link, until you stop sharing them or the share expires.
  • Agents and apps you connect (AI agents through MCP, the commonink command line, and others) act with your access, in the workspaces you allow. Whatever they read is sent to them, and to the AI provider behind them, whose own terms and privacy policies apply. You can disconnect any of them in Settings, under Agents.
  • Service providers who run Common Ink on our behalf (below).
  • The law. We may disclose information if we believe it is required by law, or to protect the rights and safety of our users, the public or us.

Service providers

  • Cloudflare hosts Common Ink and stores its data (databases and files) on its global network, which may be outside your country.
  • Google provides sign-in and, if you connect them, Calendar, Contacts and Drive. Your profile picture is loaded from Google's servers.
  • Stripe processes payments for the hosted plan. We send it your name, email address and account ID when you subscribe. Stripe's privacy policy covers the payment details you give it.

Links and embeds

When you paste a link, our servers fetch that page to build a preview, so the site sees a request from us rather than from you. For GitHub issue and pull request links, we ask GitHub's API instead. Embedded content such as YouTube videos or social posts loads from those services in your browser, and they may collect data or set cookies under their own policies.

Cookies and local storage

We only use cookies Common Ink needs to work:

  • __Host-ci_session keeps you signed in for up to 30 days, or until it's gone unused for 14.
  • __Host-ci_oauth protects the Google sign-in step and expires after 10 minutes.
  • __Host-ci_signup carries a new account through sign-up and expires after 15 minutes.
  • __Host-ci_gcal protects connecting Google Calendar, Contacts or Drive and expires after 10 minutes.

Your browser's local storage also remembers preferences such as your theme and the last note you had open. We don't use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies.

How long we keep it

  • Your account, notes and files are kept until you delete them or delete your account (in Settings, under Danger zone). If your plan lapses, nothing is deleted; your workspaces just become read-only.
  • Deleting your account cancels your subscription, deletes your personal workspace and any team workspace you're the only member of, signs you out everywhere, disconnects your agents and Google, and removes you from other teams. Notes you wrote in teams you leave stay with those teams.
  • Stripe keeps records of payments made, as tax and accounting law requires.
  • Deleted items are removed from your workspace right away. Copies in backups and storage are erased within 30 days.
  • A note's earlier versions stay in its workspace's history so changes can be undone. Ask us if you want a workspace's history erased too.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to see, correct, export or delete your personal information by writing to support@commonink.app. Depending on where you live, you may have further rights, including to object to or restrict how we use your information, and to complain to your local data protection authority. We'll respond within 30 days. You can also remove Common Ink's access in your Google Account settings.

Security

All traffic is encrypted in transit. Every request is checked against the workspaces you belong to, and uploaded files are served in a sandbox so they can't run code in your session. No system is perfectly secure; if we learn of a breach affecting your data we'll tell you promptly.

Children

Common Ink isn't intended for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes we'll tell you in the app or by email before they take effect, and update the date at the top.
