title: Open sections stay open after a rename
---
Which collapsible sections you opened in a note is remembered per note in your browser. It used to be lost when the note was renamed or moved.

1. Open [[Fold me]]. Click the arrow before **First section** to open it.
2. Change the heading to `# Fold me again` and click below it, so the note is renamed.
3. Open another note, then come back to "Fold me again". **First section** is still open.
4. Move the note to another folder (⋯ → Move) and open it again. Still open.
5. Sections you opened before this change are kept too: the first time a note opens, what was remembered under its old name moves over.
