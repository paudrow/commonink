title: Drag a note into a split
---
1. Open [[Drag me]] and star it (the ☆ in its top bar), so it shows under Favorites in the sidebar. Then open [[Drop here]].
2. Drag **Drag me** from Favorites over the note and move it around. A dashed outline shows where it'll open: "Open on the right" or "Open on the left" over each half, "Open above" near the top edge, "Open below" near the bottom edge. Drop it and the split opens there.
3. Drag the line between the panes to resize them (up and down when they're stacked), then reload: the split comes back where you left it.
4. While split, drag a card from Notes (or a starred note) onto either pane. It says "Open here" and replaces the note in that pane.
5. Close the split. With vim on, `:split` opens the side pane below and `:vsplit` beside.
