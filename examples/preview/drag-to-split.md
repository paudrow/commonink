title: Drag a note into a split
---
1. Open [[Drag me]] and star it (the ☆ in its top bar), so it shows under Favorites in the sidebar. Then open [[Drop here]].
2. Drag **Drag me** from Favorites onto the right half of the note. A dashed outline, "Open in a split", shows where the side pane will go. Drop it: the two notes are side by side.
3. Drag it again but stay on the left half: nothing lights up, and dropping does nothing.
4. While split, drag a card from Notes (or a starred note) onto the side pane. It says "Open here" and replaces the note there.
