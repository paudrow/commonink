title: A smart folder's name heads Notes
---
1. Click the **+** on the Smart folders header. Name it "Errands", pick Tag `errand`, and Save. Notes opens with **Errands** as its heading, not "Notes", and the browser tab says "Errands · Common Ink". [[Errand list]] is listed.
2. There's no **Save as smart folder** button, since these filters are already saved.
3. Change Sort to **By title**. The filters no longer match Errands, so the heading goes back to "Notes" and **Save as smart folder** comes back. Switch back to **Newest** and the page is Errands again.
4. Click **Notes** in the sidebar. The heading says "Notes".
