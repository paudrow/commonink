title: A Today page
---
1. Click **Today**, first in the sidebar (the app still opens on Notes). It has the quick-add bar, today's journal note, today's events, and what's overdue, due today and starting today. ⌘K → "Go to Today" and `/today` get you there too.
2. Open **Tasks**: just the list, with its Everyone / Assigned to me / Assigned by me filters and nothing above it. Today's tasks are in the list with the rest.
3. Back on **Today**, click a task's note to open it, then the back arrow: you're on Today again. Tick today's tasks one by one: the last one rings its box with ink, a toast says "Today's tasks are done.", and the day reads **All done for today.**
4. Under the day is **This week**: how many of 7 days you wrote on, then twelve weeks of squares and a line saying what counts. A new Preview only has today, so type a few words into any note, come back after about five seconds, and today's square is filled.
5. In any note, type `/streak` and open its settings (the sliders icon): pick **Journal** as the folder and save. The header now reads **Writing days in Journal**, counting only journal notes.
