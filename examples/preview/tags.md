1. In the sidebar's **Tags** section, open `client` with its chevron and click `acme`: Notes narrows to [[Acme kickoff]] and [[Q3 review]].
2. Hover the `globex` row and click its star: `client/globex` joins Favorites. Click the filled star to take it out again.
3. Click a `#status/waiting` chip on a card in Notes to filter by it.
4. Open the Tags page (the sliders button on the Tags header), rename `status/waiting` to `status/blocked`, then press Undo.
