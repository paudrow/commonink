# Export notes

Notes about how exporting works, embedded in [[Export sampler]].

## Summary

Print and every export start from one **static render** of the note: live preview's look, with widgets and boards as a snapshot of what they show now, and embedded notes drawn in place.

- Print is always light, with the title and date at the top and page numbers at the bottom.
- A web page export is one file, pictures and all.
