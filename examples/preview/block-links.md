title: Block links
---
Right-click in a note for the usual Cut, Copy, Paste and Select all, then short items to link to, or show elsewhere, one paragraph, one list item, or whatever you've highlighted.

1. Open [[Menu plan]]. Its link goes to one paragraph of [[Recipe ideas]], the first embed shows just that paragraph, the second just one list item (with the item under it), and the last a highlighted stretch: from the soups paragraph through the Pickles item, heading included.
2. Click the link. Recipe ideas opens at the soups paragraph.
3. In Recipe ideas, right-click the last paragraph ("A plain paragraph…"). The menu reads **Cut ⌘X**, **Copy ⌘C** (both greyed out, since nothing is selected), **Paste ⌘V**, **Select all ⌘A**, then **Copy link to this note**, **Copy link to this paragraph** and **Copy embed of this paragraph**. Hover an item for a one-line explanation.
4. Pick **Copy link to this paragraph**. A toast says "Link copied". The paragraph gets a short code at its end that hides once the cursor leaves the line. ⌘Z takes it back in one step.
5. Right-click "Granola": the items say **list item**. Right-click the `## Pantry` heading: **Copy link to this heading**, which adds nothing to the note.
6. Highlight from the middle of "Soups for the winter…" down into "Bread on Sundays", then right-click inside the highlight. Cut and Copy work, and the last two items say **Copy link to selection** and **Copy embed of selection**. Pick the embed, open [[Menu plan]], and paste at the end: the embed shows the paragraph, the Pantry heading and the list item. Partial lines count as the whole paragraph or item.
7. Copy something, right-click and pick **Paste**. If the browser asks to allow reading the clipboard, allow it; if it refuses, a toast says to press ⌘V instead.
8. Without the mouse: press Shift+F10 (or the Menu key) in a paragraph. The menu opens at the cursor; arrows skip the greyed-out items, Enter picks one, Escape closes it.
9. Shift+right-click still shows the browser's own menu.
10. ⌘K has **Copy link to this paragraph or selection** and **Copy embed of this paragraph or selection**; the web address of the note is now **Copy web address of this note**.
11. The side panel's Backlinks for Recipe ideas counts all of Menu plan's links and embeds.
