title: The ::streak widget is gone
---
Writing days now show in one place only: the week card on Today.

1. In any note, type `/` and look through the widgets: there's no **Writing days** (streak) entry.
2. Open **Today**: the week card and its 12-week heatmap are still there.
3. In a note, add `::calendar`: the month still shows how many entries you wrote, without a "day streak".
