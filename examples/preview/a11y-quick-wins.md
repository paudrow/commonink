title: Accessibility quick wins
---
Small fixes from an accessibility audit. Most are for keyboard and screen reader users.

1. Press Tab until the focus reaches the sidebar. Focused rows (folders, tags, favorites) and section toggles now have a solid ring in your ink color, where before it was a faint tint. The same goes for Notes card actions and Assets cards.
2. Open Tasks. The quick-add placeholder ("Add a task…") is easier to read. Trash paths and the contact @handles have the same darker grey.
3. Open a note and press ⌘⇧Enter for focus mode. The status bar fades. Tab to the gear: the bar comes back to full strength while it has the keyboard, not just on hover.
4. With a screen reader on, type in a note. You no longer hear "Edited… Saving… Saved" at every pause, only "Not saved" if a save fails.
5. A folder or tag with children has a chevron. A screen reader now announces it as "Show the folders in Projects, collapsed" (or expanded).
