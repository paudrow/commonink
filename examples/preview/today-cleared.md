title: Celebrate a clear Today
---
The sample notes have plenty due today, so this is easiest in a fresh workspace: workspace menu at the top of the sidebar → **New team workspace…**.

1. Open **Today** and add two tasks with the quick-add bar: "Buy milk today" and "Call the bank today". Both show under Today.
2. Tick one: the usual "Done" toast. Tick the other: drops of ink burst from its box, and a toast says "Today's tasks are done." with a friendly second line. Today now reads **All done for today.**
3. Add and tick another task due today: no second celebration today in this browser. Clear `commonink.todayCleared` from local storage to see it again.
4. With Reduce motion on, you get the toast without the burst. Ticking the last task from its checkbox inside a note celebrates too.
