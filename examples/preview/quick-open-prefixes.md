title: Quick open prefixes
---
Quick open (⌘K) can now jump to headings, people, tags and folders, not just notes.

1. Press ⌘K. Under the list, one line says what you can start with: `#`, `@`, `tag:`, `/` and `>`.
2. Open **Tips**, press ⌘K and type `#`. The list shows the note's headings. Type `keys` and press Enter: the note scrolls to the Keys heading.
3. Press ⌘K and type `tag:start`. Press Enter. Notes opens, narrowed to #start.
4. Press ⌘K and type `/proj` (or `folder:proj`). Press Enter. Notes opens, narrowed to the Projects folder. Saved smart folders show under the folders.
5. Make a contact first if you have none (Contacts, New contact). Press ⌘K and type `@` and part of their name. Press Enter. Their contact page opens.
6. Press ⌘K, type `#nothing` and press Shift+Enter. No note called "#nothing" is made.
