title: Sidebar polish and new tags
---
1. Look at the sidebar's section heads. Smart folders, Folders and Tags each end in the same +. Hover each one: "New smart folder", "New folder", "New tag". Tags' sliders button sits to the left of its +.
2. Click the + on **Tags**, type `#areas/health` and press Enter. Areas and Health appear under Tags with no count.
3. Open [[Plan the tags]] and type `#are` at the end of its last line. The suggestions offer `areas` and `areas/health`. Pick `areas/health`: the tag now counts 1 note. Delete it from the note again and the tag leaves the sidebar, like any tag once a note has used it.
4. Add `someday/maybe` the same way. Hover its row and click the bin: it goes, and Undo brings it back. Open the Tags page (the sliders), rename `someday` to `later`, then press Undo.
5. For the empty sections, make a fresh workspace from the account menu ("New team workspace…"). Favorites and Smart folders each say what goes there, starting where the rows' icons start under Tags (`start`). Open Folders to see its line too. Try it at a phone's width (the ☰ drawer) and in dark mode.
