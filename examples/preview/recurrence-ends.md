title: Repeats that end
---
1. In [[Loan and lessons]], the loan's repeat chip reads "6th · 3 left". Tick it: the next one is due on the 6th with 2 left. Tick that and the next: the last one makes no new task.
2. The pottery class repeats weekly until a few weeks from now (its chip says "until …"). Tick it until the next date would pass that day: then no new one appears.
3. Click a repeat chip and choose **More options…**: the **Ends** row sets Never, On a date, or After a number of times; the summary and the next dates stop where it ends.
4. On the loan choose **Skip this one**: it moves to the next date and uses one of its times.
5. In the quick-add bar, try `standup every week until dec 1`, `rent every month for 6 months` or `stretch daily 10 times`.
