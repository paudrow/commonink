1. Open [[Launch board]]. Drag "Record the demo video" into the empty **Review** column, then drag cards onto the top and bottom half of another card: the blue bar shows where each lands.
2. Click the name of the "Pricing tiers" card to open that note, then go back. ⌘-click (Ctrl-click off a Mac) opens it to the side where split view is in the branch.
3. Drag "Water the office plants" into **Done**: it's ticked with today's date, and a repeating card's next occurrence lands at the top of Backlog once repeating tasks are in.
4. Fold **Done** with its arrow: the column keeps its width and header. Unfold it.
5. Open a column's ⋯ menu and pick a colour; pick the crossed-out swatch to take it off.
6. Press `<>` (Edit as text), change `## Review` to `### Review`, and move the cursor below the board: Review is still a column.
7. Still in the text, type a plain line under `## Doing`, then leave the board: a banner names the line. Try **Make this a card**.
8. In a new note, type `/kanban` and press Enter for a starter board.
9. Open [[Board from another note]]: the `::view{show=board note="Launch board"}` embed is live.
