title: Pages past Today, Notes and Tasks fold under More
---
The top of the sidebar is now Today, Notes and Tasks, then a **More** row.

1. Look at the sidebar: under Tasks there's **More**, folded. Contacts, Calendar, Assets, History and Shared with me are inside it.
2. Click **More**. The pages open below it. Click it again to fold them away; the choice is remembered.
3. With More folded, press ⌘K and open **History**. History shows under More while you're on it, then folds away when you leave.
4. Open Settings (⌘,) → **Sidebar** and turn on **Keep Calendar up top**. Calendar moves up beside Today, Notes and Tasks.
