title: Pages past Today, Notes and Tasks fold under More
---
The top of the sidebar is now Today, Notes, Tasks and Calendar, then a **More** row.

1. Look at the sidebar: under Calendar there's **More**, folded. Contacts, Assets, History and Shared with me are inside it.
2. Click **More**. The pages open below it. Click it again to fold them away; the choice is remembered.
3. With More folded, press ⌘K and open **History**. History shows up top, above More, while you're on it, then folds away when you leave.
4. Open Settings (⌘,) → **Sidebar**. **Keep Calendar up top** is on; turn it off and Calendar moves under More. Turn on **Keep Contacts up top** and Contacts joins the top.
