title: Turn gamification off for a workspace
---
1. Open Settings (⌘,) and search "gamification", or scroll to **Workspace → Unlock as you go**. It's on.
2. Turn it off. The sidebar shows Contacts, Calendar, Assets and Smart folders straight away, the **Always show** pins and **Shortcut tips** leave Settings, and every ink under Appearance → **Ink** can be picked, with no locks.
3. Tick the last of Today's tasks: no celebration. Click Search three times: no tip.
4. Open the workspace menu → workspace settings: the same switch is there for owners, and Activity says you changed it. Sign in as someone who isn't an owner (or open a workspace where you're an editor): Settings shows the switch greyed out, saying only an owner can change it.
5. Turn it back on. Empty sections wait until they're in use again, and inks you've earned in this browser are still yours.
