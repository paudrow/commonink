title: Accessibility
---
1. Open [[Contrast check]], then switch themes with the moon button at the bottom right. The dates, counts, `Low` chip, code comment and sidebar headings (FAVORITES, FOLDERS) read clearly in both. In dark, the timer's **Start** button has dark text on the purple.
2. In **Notes**, click a card's title: the note opens. ⌘-click a title (Ctrl-click off a Mac) to open it to the side, or middle-click it for a new tab. A click on the card's text still expands it.
3. From the Notes filter box, press Tab until a card's title has the focus ring: the card lights up as with j/k. Tab to the chevron at the card's right, press Enter to expand it and Space to collapse it.
4. Turn on VoiceOver (⌘F5) and move through the sidebar and the top bar: the entry for the page you're on says "current page", Active / Archived / All say "selected" or not, and the star, archive and split buttons say their names.
5. Turn on Reduce motion (System Settings › Accessibility › Display) and press ⌘\: the info panel snaps shut instead of sliding.
