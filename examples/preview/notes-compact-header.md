title: Compact Notes header, and j/k never hides a note under it
---
1. Open **Notes**. The header is one row of filters under the search: the tabs, **Tag**, a folder dropdown (it replaces the row of folder chips), and **Newest** on the right. The "Notes" title scrolls away with the list; only the search and filters stay at the top.
2. **Save as smart folder** shows only once a search, tag, folder or sort is set. Pick a folder from the dropdown to see it appear.
3. Click the list, press `j` a dozen times, then `k` back up. The focused card always stops fully below the header, never under it.
4. Press `x` to select a card, then `k` again: the card also stays clear of the "1 selected" bar.
5. In **History**, move with `j`/`k` through a long list: a row no longer hides under the sticky day heading.
