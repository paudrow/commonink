title: Tasks on phones and touch screens
---
1. Open this Preview on a phone, or on a touch tablet. Go to **Tasks**.
2. Each task shows its ⚙ (fields) and ↗ (go to note) buttons. Before, they were invisible but still tappable, so a tap near the right edge of a row could open a menu you couldn't see. On a phone, "Open to the side" is gone, since a phone shows one pane.
3. On a phone, Tasks and Tags use the screen's width: 16px margins like Notes, not 32px. Task text wraps less.
4. On a touch tablet, in **Notes**, tap near the right end of a card you're not on. Nothing happens except the card opening. Before, you could hit an invisible Archive or Delete.
