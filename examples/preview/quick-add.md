1. On **Tasks**, type `pay rent every month on the 1st #home` in the bar at the top. "every month on the 1st" lights up and the chips show what will be written. Press Enter: it goes under Tasks in today's journal note.
2. Type `call mom next fri`, then click inside the lit "next fri": it goes back to plain words.
3. Type `water plants every 3 days after done → [[Home]]`: it's filed in [[Home]] instead.
4. Open [[Home]] and press ⌘⇧. (Ctrl+Shift+. elsewhere), in any Vim mode: the bar floats over the note, and Tab switches where the task goes between today's journal and Home. With Vim on, the bar is Vim too.
5. On any task's ⚙ menu choose **Move to…** and pick a note.
