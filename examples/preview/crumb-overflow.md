title: Long folder paths in the top bar
---
1. Open [[Roadmap review with a fairly long name]]. Its path in the top bar runs through three folders.
2. Narrow the window, or open the side panel with ⌘\. The folders shrink first, to "Q…" and "E…", then the note's name. Nothing runs under "Saved" or the icons, and every button stays the same size.
3. Hover a shortened folder: its tooltip starts with the folder's full name. Click it to move the note, as before.
