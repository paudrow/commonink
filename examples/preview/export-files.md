title: Word and zip exports
---
1. Open [[Export sampler]], then **Share → Word**. Open `Export sampler.docx` in Word, Google Docs or Pages. It's the note in Word's own structure: headings, task boxes, the board as a table, code in its colors, the alerts, the picture and the diagram. Formulas are their LaTeX.
2. **Share → Markdown** on the sampler: since it shows a picture, you get `Export sampler.zip` with the note and `Print badge.svg`, in their folder.
3. In the sidebar under **Folders**, open **Try** and hover **Word and zip exports**. Press the download arrow to get `Word and zip exports.zip` with [[Zip me]] and [[Zip me too]]. In it, the link to Zip me too is still `[[Zip me too]]`, and the link to the sampler (left out) is its web address.
4. On **Notes**, select two notes (`x`, or their check), then **Export** in the bar that appears: `Notes.zip`.
5. ⌘K, then "export all": **Export all notes (.zip)** downloads the whole workspace, Archive included.
6. For agents: MCP `export_note` here gives a note as Markdown, or a folder (or `/`) as a zip. Asked for Word, it says to use the Share menu.
