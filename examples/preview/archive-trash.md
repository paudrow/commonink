title: Archive and Trash as tabs of Notes
---
1. Look at the sidebar, then open the ☰ drawer at a phone's width: no Archive or Trash, and the foot is just New note.
2. In Notes, find [[Old launch checklist]] and press `e` on its card (or open it and use Archive in the top bar). Click the **Archive** tab: the note is there, and the line under the tabs says what Archive is for. The address is `/archive`.
3. Back on **Notes**, delete [[Scratch launch notes]] from its card's bin. Click the **Trash** tab: it's there with Restore and Delete forever, and the line says when it goes for good.
4. On the Notes tab, type "launch" in the filter. Under the list, a line like "Also 1 in Archive and 1 in Trash." names the matches in the other tabs. Click each to go there with the search kept.
5. Try the old ways in: ⌘K "Go to Archive" and "Go to Trash", `:archive` in vim, and the address `/notes?scope=archived`, which lands on `/archive`.
6. Restore the scratch note from Trash, and press `e` on the checklist in Archive to unarchive it.
