title: Vim keys live in Settings, on in Previews
---
1. Open [[Getting started]]. Vim keys start on in Previews: the left of the status bar says NORMAL, and there's no **Vim keys** chip at the right.
2. Open Settings (⌘,) and untick **Vim keys**. NORMAL goes. Tick it again and it comes back. ⌘⇧P **Turn vim keys off** / **on** does the same.
3. Reload: your choice stays. On commonink.app a new user still starts with Vim keys off.
