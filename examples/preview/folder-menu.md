title: Right-click menus in the sidebar and Notes
---
1. Hover a folder under **Folders** in the sidebar: only the + (new note) shows now.
2. Right-click the folder: a menu offers New note here, Export as .zip, Share… and Delete….
3. Right-click a tag, a smart folder, a favorite, or a card in Notes: each has its own menu (a card's has Open, Open to the side, Star, Select, Export, Archive and Delete).
4. From the keyboard: Tab to a sidebar row (or move to a card in Notes with j/k) and press Shift+F10 or the Menu key. Use the arrows to move and Escape to close.
5. At a phone's width (or on a touch screen), each sidebar row shows ⋯, which opens the same menu.
