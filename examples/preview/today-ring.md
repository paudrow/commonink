title: The Today ring
---
A thin ring beside **Today** in the sidebar fills as you tick today's tasks (overdue, due or starting today) and closes when Today is clear.

1. Look at **Today** in the sidebar: a small ring sits at the right of it. Hover it for "N of M of today's tasks done". The sample notes have tasks due today, so it starts partly empty.
2. Open **Try/The Today ring/Ring practice** and tick "Reply to Dana". The ring fills a little more, without reloading. Untick it and the ring gives it back.
3. Open **Today**: a larger ring sits beside the date, with "N of M done". Tick everything left there. Both rings close into a full circle as the last one goes, the big one says "All done", and Today reads "All done for today."
4. Switch the theme in Settings (⌘,): the ring reads in light and dark. With Reduce motion on it jumps instead of sliding.
5. Turn off **Settings → Workspace → Unlock as you go**. The ring is gone from the sidebar and from the Today page. Turn it back on and both return.
