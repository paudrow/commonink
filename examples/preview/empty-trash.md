title: An empty Trash hides its search box
---
With nothing in Trash, there's nothing to search, so the Trash tab shows just its tabs and says it's empty.

1. Click the **Trash** tab. With Trash empty, there's no search box or filters, only "Trash is empty".
2. Go back to **Notes** and delete a note (Delete key on its card). Open **Trash** again: the search box and filters are back.
3. Restore it with `r`. Trash is empty again, and the search box goes away.
