title: Your week on Today
---
This Preview's notes were all made today, so last week's recap counts the three tasks [[Last week's errands]] ticked a week ago. In a workspace you used last week it also counts the notes you made and edited, agents' edits for you, your busiest day and the note you came back to most.

1. Open **Today**. Under the day, where Writing streak was, a **Last week** card with its dates says "You wrote on 0 of 7 days" and "3 tasks ticked off", with the 12-week heatmap under it.
2. Click **Save as journal note**: `Journal/Week of <last Monday>` opens with the same lines. Back on Today the card is **This week**, with today counted once you've written something (give it about five seconds).
3. Reload Today: still This week until next Monday. Clear `commonink.weekRecap` from local storage to see Last week again, then dismiss it with its ✕ instead: back to This week.
4. Turn off Settings (⌘,) → Workspace → **Unlock as you go** and open Today: there's no week card at all. A `::streak` widget in a note still works.
