title: More verbs in the command palette
---
1. Press ⌘⇧P (Ctrl+Shift+P off a Mac) and type `star tag`. Pick **Star or unstar a tag…**. The palette lists the tags. Pick `#tour`: it shows up under Favorites. Do it again to take it off.
2. Click `#tour` in the sidebar's Tags so Notes shows only that tag. Open ⌘⇧P and type `tour`. You'll see **Star #tour** and **Rename #tour…** for the tag you're looking at.
3. Pick **Rename #tour…**. The palette asks the new name, with `tour` typed in. Press Esc to leave it as it is.
4. Go back to Notes with `#tour` still picked. In ⌘⇧P, pick **Save these filters as a smart folder**. The palette asks its name. With no filters on, the command isn't offered.
5. In ⌘⇧P, pick **New contact…**. The palette asks the name, then an email and a company. **Import contacts (.vcf or .csv)…** is there too.
6. Open [[Tips]], then ⌘⇧P → **Label this version…** and give it a name. Type a few words in the note. Now ⌘⇧P → **Restore to a version…** lists its labels; pick one to restore it, with Undo.
7. On a note with no labels, **Restore to a version…** says how to make one.
8. Archive a note, then open it: ⌘⇧P shows **Unarchive note** in place of Archive note.
