title: More verbs in the command palette
---
1. Press ⌘⇧P (Ctrl+Shift+P off a Mac) and type `star tag`. Pick **Star or unstar a tag…**. A tag list opens under the search box. Pick `#tour`: it shows up under Favorites. Do it again to take it off.
2. Click `#tour` in the sidebar's Tags so Notes shows only that tag. Open ⌘⇧P and type `tour`. You'll see **Star #tour** and **Rename #tour…** for the tag you're looking at.
3. Pick **Rename #tour…**. The Tags page opens with `#tour` ready to rename. Press Esc to leave it as it is.
4. Go back to Notes with `#tour` still picked. In ⌘⇧P, pick **Save these filters as a smart folder**. The same form as the page's "Save as smart folder" button opens. With no filters on, the command isn't offered.
5. In ⌘⇧P, pick **New contact…**. Contacts opens with the New contact form. **Import contacts (.vcf or .csv)…** is there too.
6. Open [[Tips]], then ⌘⇧P → **Label this version…** and give it a name. Type a few words in the note. Now ⌘⇧P → **Restore to a version…** opens the note's History with that label compared with now, and its Restore button.
7. On a note with no labels, **Restore to a version…** opens its History and says how to make one.
8. Archive a note, then open it: ⌘⇧P shows **Unarchive note** in place of Archive note.
