title: Your own archive folder, and backlinks without archived notes

1. In the sidebar's Folders, there's no **4. Archive**: it counts as the archive now, the way Archive/ does. Click the **Archive** tab in Notes: [[Old launch brief]] is there, from 4. Archive.
2. Open [[Launch brief]] and look at Backlinks in the side panel. [[Launch notes]] links to it, and under it a line says "1 more from archived note". Click it to see [[Old launch brief]], and again to hide it.
3. Search for "launch brief" (⌘K): the old copy in 4. Archive isn't in the results.
4. Open [[Wrap-up meeting]] and archive it from the top bar. It moves to `4. Archive/Try/…/Wrap-up meeting.md`, not a new Archive/ folder. Unarchive it and it goes back where it was.
5. Open [[Old launch brief]] itself: its backlinks show everything, archived or not, since there they're the context.
