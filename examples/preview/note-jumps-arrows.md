title: Back and forward arrows
---
1. Open [[Trip plan]] and follow [[Flights]], then [[Airport transfer]]. The ← and → at the left of the top bar go back and forward, like a browser's. They work the same as ⌘[ and ⌘] (Ctrl-O and Ctrl-I in vim). Hover one to see its shortcut.
2. At the start or end of the trail, the arrow that has nowhere to go is greyed out, and clicking it does nothing.
3. Right-click ←, or press and hold it, to see where back goes: Flights, Trip plan… Pick one to jump straight there. → lists what's ahead the same way.
4. With the keyboard: Tab to ← and press Enter. The focus stays on it, so Enter again keeps going back.
5. Split the view (⌘-click a link). Each pane's bar has the same arrows, for that pane only. The top bar's arrows follow whichever pane has the focus.
6. On a phone, the arrows are at the top, next to the menu. → only shows when there's somewhere to go forward.
