title: Footnotes inside alerts
---
1. Open [[GitHub markdown sampler]]. The Note alert's text now has a footnote, a 💡 emoji and a key. In the editor, the footnote's number is raised, and hovering it shows the footnote.
2. Embed the sampler in a note (`![[GitHub markdown sampler]]`), or open its card on the Notes page. There, too, the alert's footnote is a raised 1 that links to the footnote at the end, and the emoji and key render. Before this fix, rendered notes showed it as `[^alert]`.
