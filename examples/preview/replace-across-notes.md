title: Replace across notes
---
Find and replace in every note at once, with a preview first and one Undo.

1. Press ⌘⇧P and run **Replace across notes…**. A page opens with Find and Replace with fields.
2. Type `agent` in Find and `assistant` in Replace with. Below, each note that would change is listed with its lines: `agent` struck through in red, `assistant` beside it in green. The line above says how many places in how many notes.
3. Tick **Whole word**. "agents" drops out and the count goes down. Pick a folder in the menu to narrow it to that folder.
4. Click a line to open the note there. Come back with the back arrow.
5. Click **Replace all**. A toast says "Replaced 10 places in 5 notes" and the list empties. History shows one change per note.
6. Click **Undo** in the toast. Every note goes back, and the list fills again.
