title: Repeating tasks
---
1. In [[Bills and chores]], tick **Pay rent**: it's marked done today, and the next one appears right below, due on the 6th whatever day you paid.
2. Tick the open **Give the dog her pill**: the next one is due a month from today, because it repeats after it's done (`rec:after-1m`; the ticked line above shows the last dose).
3. Click the repeat chip on **Book club notes** ("1st & 3rd Tue") and choose **More options…**: see the plain-English summary and the next dates, then change it to the last Friday.
4. On **Payroll**'s repeat chip choose **Skip this one**: its due date moves to the next last Friday without ticking it.
5. On **Renew the domain** ("Day 50") choose **Stop repeating**.
6. **Quarterly sync** repeats by a raw `RRULE:`; its repeat chip shows the summary and opens as text.
7. Tick a repeating task and untick it straight away (or press ⌘Z in the note): the next one goes away again.
