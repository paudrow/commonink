title: Split view
---
1. Open [[Split view notes]]. Hover the [[Launch board]] link: a split button appears at its end. Click it and the board opens beside the note. The web links end in ↗ and open in your browser instead.
2. Press ⌘K, type "Pricing", and press ⌘↵ (Ctrl+↵ off a Mac): it opens to the side. Plain ↵ opens it in place.
3. Drag a `[[link]]` from a note, a board's link card, a task row or a Notes card to the right edge of the window, and drop it on "Open in split view".
4. Put the cursor on a `[[link]]` and press ⌘⌥↵ (Ctrl+Alt+↵ off a Mac). With vim on, `gs` in normal mode does the same.
5. ⌘-click (Ctrl-click off a Mac) a link, a card's name on [[Launch board]], a task's ↗, a starred note or a Notes card: each opens to the side. While split, a link clicked in one pane opens in the other.
6. ⌘⌥\ closes the focused pane and splits again; ⌘⌥[ and ⌘⌥] move the focus. Drag the line between the panes, then reload: the layout comes back.
7. With [[Launch board]] open in one pane, open it from the other (⌘K): the pane that has it takes the focus instead of opening it twice.
