title: Split view
---
1. Open [[Launch board]] and ⌘-click (Ctrl-click off a Mac) the name of its "Pricing tiers" card: the note opens beside the board. Click a card's name again: it opens in the other pane, and the board stays put.
2. Press ⌘⌥\ to close the focused pane, then ⌘⌥\ again to split: the side pane gets back the note it had.
3. Press ⌘⌥[ and ⌘⌥] to move the focus left and right. The outline and backlinks on the right follow it.
4. Drag the line between the panes to resize them, then reload the page: both notes and the width come back.
5. ⌘-click other things to open them to the side: a card on the Notes page, a `[[link]]` in [[Split view notes]], a task's ↗ on the Tasks page, a starred note in the sidebar, a backlink. Or drag a note from the sidebar to the right edge.
6. With [[Launch board]] open in one pane, open it from the other (the palette, ⌘K): the pane that has it takes the focus instead of opening it twice.
