title: Task details as tokens
---
1. Open [[Errands]]. Off the cursor's line, each task's tokens are chips. Click **High** and pick Low, click a date and pick Tomorrow; ⌘Z undoes each.
2. Click into "Call the plumber": a faint `due · repeat · @ · # · !` follows it. Click `due` and pick a day, then `#` and add a tag. The ⚙ at the end of the line (or ⌘.) lists every field.
3. At the end of any task line type ` due:` and pick a suggestion; ` @` suggests people already on tasks.
4. On the **Tasks** page, click a task's words to edit them in place (Enter saves, Esc cancels). The ↗ on a row opens the note at that line.
5. In **Notes**, the Errands card shows the same chips. Click one to edit it right there.
