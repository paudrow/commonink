# Esc then dd

Previews start with Vim keys on, for testing; on commonink.app a new user starts with them off. Open Settings (⌘,) and untick **Vim keys** to see the new-user default.

Click at the end of this line, press Esc, then type dd.

With Vim keys off, you get the letters "dd" and every line stays put. Delete them and carry on.

Now tick **Vim keys** in Settings again. A NORMAL or INSERT badge appears at the left of the status bar. Click at the end of this line, press Esc, then type dd: the line goes, as it should in Vim. Press u to bring it back.

Reload the page: your choice stays.
