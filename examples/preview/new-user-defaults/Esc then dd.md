# Esc then dd

Click at the end of this line, press Esc, then type dd.

With Vim keys off (the new default), you get the letters "dd" and every line stays put. Delete them and carry on.

Now click **Vim keys: off** in the status bar so it reads **Vim keys: on**. A NORMAL or INSERT badge appears at the left of the status bar. Click at the end of this line, press Esc, then type dd: the line goes, as it should in Vim. Press u to bring it back.

Reload the page: your choice stays.
