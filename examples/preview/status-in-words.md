title: No dots when all is well
---
The save dot in the top bar and the connection dot in the status bar are gone. While your notes are saved and the app can reach the server, the status bar says nothing. When something is off, it says so in words.

1. Open any note and look at the top bar. There's no "Saved" with a dot any more. Look at the bottom right of the status bar. There's no green dot either.
2. Type a few words. Nothing flickers: a quick save comes and goes without a word. If a save takes longer than a second, "Saving…" shows in the status bar until it's done.
3. Lose the connection (on a laptop, turn off Wi-Fi). Red text shows in the status bar: "Offline · changes will save when back". Hover it for more. Keep typing.
4. Reconnect. The text goes away, and what you typed while offline is saved.
5. If a save fails while you're online, the status bar says "Not saved · retrying" until a retry works.
6. With a screen reader on, going offline, coming back and a failed save are each announced once.
