title: Advanced search in Notes
---
1. Open **Notes** and type `budget` in **Filter notes…**. [[Garden]], [[Budget review]] and [[Launch plan]] are among the matches. The plain filter is unchanged.
2. Click **Advanced** in the filter box (or press ⌘⌥F, Ctrl+Alt+F off a Mac, or pick **Advanced search…** in ⌘K). The view editor opens with **Words** `budget` already in it, and its **Query** line reads `q=budget`.
3. In **Tags**, pick `work`. The count narrows (Budget review and Launch plan, not Garden), and so does the Notes list behind the dialog, and the filter bar's Tag chip says `#work`. Change **Sort** to **By title**: the Notes sort menu follows.
4. Press **Escape** (or Cancel). Notes goes back to just `budget`, as it was.
5. Open Advanced again, make the **Query** box `q="budget -tag=home"` and press **Enter** (Done). The filter box now reads `budget -tag=home`, the one query both edit.
6. Open Advanced once more and click **Save as view**. A name is suggested; type "Budget, not home" and press Enter. It's in the sidebar under Views and Notes is headed by its name.
7. In Trash the **Advanced** button isn't there: Trash's filter is words only.
