---
title: Shared with me looks like the other pages
---
This Preview shares [[Plan for Sam]] (editor), [[Read only for Sam]] (viewer) and the folder **Shared folder** with a second person, Sam Dev.

1. In a private window, open `/auth/dev?as=sam` on this Preview, then **Shared with me** in the sidebar. The title and the line under it sit in the same column, with the same margins, as Tags, Contacts and Tasks.
2. The notes are listed under the name of the workspace they're from, with a count. Each row shows the note's name, its path, and **Can edit** or **View only**. Hover a row: it highlights like a row in Contacts. Click one to open it on its own page.
3. Narrow the window to a phone's width: the title fits on one line with a gutter on each side, and long names and paths end in "…" instead of wrapping.
4. Sign in as someone nothing is shared with (`/auth/dev?as=nobody`) and open `/shared`: the empty page has the same icon, heading and text layout as an empty Notes or Contacts page.
