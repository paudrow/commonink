title: Dates typed in the editor
---
1. Open [[Weekend errands]] and click the empty task at the bottom. Type `Buy milk tomorrow`. "tomorrow" gets a dotted underline, and beside the ⚙ a dashed box shows the chip it will become ("Tomorrow") with a **Tab** key.
2. Press Enter (or the Up arrow). The line becomes `Buy milk due:…` and shows a due chip. A toast says "Buy milk: due Tomorrow". Press ⌘Z (Ctrl+Z off a Mac): the words come back, and the cursor stays where it went.
3. On a new task, type `Water the ferns every week` and press Enter: it's due today and repeats weekly.
4. Type `Read the Monday memo` and press Enter. "Monday" sits inside the sentence, so it stays words. Go back to the line: Tab right after "Monday", or a click on the dashed box, makes it a date if you want one.
5. **Plan tomorrow's party** is never read as a date. **Return the library books friday** was already there, so passing through its line changes nothing. Click the dashed box beside its ⚙ to set it.
6. Type `Call the dentist friday`, press Enter, and click **Undo** on the toast: "friday" is back as words.
7. Press `?` outside the editor for the shortcut sheet: its Tab line says what Tab and leaving the line do.
