title: Late ticks on repeating tasks
---
1. Open [[Late chores]]. Every task there repeats and is overdue.
2. Tick **Water the plants** (daily, 15 days late): the next one is due today, not 14 days ago.
3. Tick **Take out the recycling** (weekly, 17 days late): the next one is due later this week on the same weekday as before.
4. Tick **Clean the gutters** (`rec:2w`, 40 days late): the next one is 2 days from now, keeping its every-other-week rhythm rather than counting two weeks from today.
5. Tick **Review the budget**: its `start:` moves with its due date, and `times:4` counts down to 3, one per tick.
6. Untick one straight after ticking it: the added task goes away again.
7. On **Call grandma**'s repeat chip, the "· next" date reads today or later. Choose **Skip this one**: the due date moves to the next Monday or Thursday on or after today.
