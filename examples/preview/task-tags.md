title: A task's tag tags the task, not its note
---
1. In the sidebar's Tags, click **#areas/podcast** (open **areas** first). Notes lists [[Podcast plan]] only. [[Open tasks]] isn't there, though one of its tasks carries the tag, and its card shows no tag chips.
2. Click **#areas/home**. No note has it, only a task, so the row shows a task count and opens Tasks narrowed to that one task.
3. Open the quick-add bar (⌘⇧. or Ctrl+Shift+.) and add `Call the bank @3pm → [[Open tasks]]`. In [[Open tasks]] the task lands under the other two, above the `---` footer, and `@3pm` stays words, not a person chip.
4. On a task line in any note, type `\@home`. It stays the word "@home", not a person.
