title: Contacts, assignees and humans
---
Contacts, a task's assignee and History's human changes each have one name now.

1. Open **Contacts** in the sidebar. The box above the list says "Filter contacts…" (it used to say "Search people…"). Type part of a name: the list narrows.
2. Press ⌘K and type `@`. The list is headed **Contacts**, and the hint under it reads `@` contacts.
3. Open any note and type `@` and part of a name on an empty line. The menu's first section is **Contacts**.
4. On a task line (`- [ ] Call the bank`), press ⌘. (Ctrl+. off a Mac). The menu's row is **Assignee** (it used to say "Person"). Pick it: the popover is called Assignee too.
5. Type `/` in a note, pick **Tasks**, and open the Group menu: the last choice is **By assignee**.
6. Open **History**. The chips are Everyone, **Humans** and Agents.
7. Open **Calendar** and make an event: the row for who's invited still says **Guests**. That's who is invited to one event, not your contacts.
