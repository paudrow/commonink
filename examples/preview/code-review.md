1. Open Notes and expand the card for [[Checkbox mix]] (click its title). The numbered and quoted checkboxes are greyed out. Tick "Second bullet task": that line, and only that line, gets `[x]` in the note.
2. Open Tasks. [[Tasks in code]] shows one task, "The real task", under "After the code". The task inside the code block isn't listed.
3. In the quick-add bar at the top of Tasks, type "Water the demo ferns tomorrow" and press Enter twice fast. Today's journal note holds the task once.
4. Still on Tasks, press Tab until a task's checkbox shows a focus ring, then press Space. It ticks, and the focus moves to the next task. Tab once more to that task's ⚙ (sliders) button and press Enter: the menu opens with the focus in it. Press Escape, and the focus is back on the ⚙.
5. Open [[Repeating board]] and drag "Pay the demo rent" into Done. It's done there, and next month's card waits at the top of To do, once. Press ⌘Z (Ctrl-Z off a Mac) once, and both changes go.
6. Split view: in [[Repeating board]], ⌘-click (Ctrl-click off a Mac) the link to Board elsewhere. It opens to the side with the same board. Add a card on the side pane's board, and the main pane shows it right away.
7. Split view again: from [[Repeating board]], ⌘-click the link to Embeds the board. Type a few words at the end of the line with the links. The embed in the side pane shows them once the note saves.
8. Open the Tags page, Tab to a tag's rename button (the pencil) and press Enter, then Escape. The focus goes back to the tag, not the top of the page.
