title: The logo takes your ink
---
1. Open Settings (⌘,) → Appearance → **Ink** and pick any ink you've earned (the sample notes earn a few here).
2. The logo at the top of the sidebar turns that color at once, and so does the icon in the browser tab.
3. Reload: the sidebar logo draws in the ink straight away. Switch to dark: the logo keeps the same shade.
4. Pick **Indigo** again: the logo and tab icon go back to the usual indigo.
