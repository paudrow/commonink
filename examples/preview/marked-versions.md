title: Marked versions
---
1. Open [[Proposal]] and press **Marked versions** in the top bar (the bookmark). Its three marks are listed newest first: **Agreed**, **Sent to Alex** and **v1**.
2. Pick **Agreed**. History opens on the note, with the marks as pins among its changes, and compares **Agreed** with the note now. The rewrite after it dropped two scope items, the timeline and the payment terms.
3. Change **Compare with** to **“v1”** to see everything that changed between the first draft and the agreed version.
4. Press **Restore to this version**. The note goes back to **Agreed** as one change, which you can undo from the toast. The pin now says **now**.
5. Rename a mark with the pencil (say, "Agreed with Alex"), and delete **v1** with the bin. Only the name goes: the note and its History stay as they were.
6. Back in the note, ⌘K and type "mark" (or `:mark v4` with Vim keys on): **Mark this version…** names the note as it is now. In History, **Mark this version…** on any change names the version right after it.
7. The **People** and **AI** filters in History still work, and the pins stay among the changes.
8. Delete the note, then open **Trash** and choose **Delete forever**: the dialog says its marked versions go too.
