title: Commands, quick open and a shortcut sheet
---
1. Press ⌘P (Ctrl+P off a Mac) and type `theme`: quick open finds notes only, like [[Theme ideas]]. ⌘K does the same.
2. Press ⌘⇧P (Ctrl+Shift+P): the palette switches to commands, with `>` typed for you. Type `theme` and press ↵ and the theme flips. Each command shows its shortcut on the right. In quick open, typing `>` does the same, and ⌘P switches back.
3. Fuzzy works in commands: `>tgthm` finds Toggle theme, and `>dark` finds it too.
4. Open [[Command palette notes]], then ⌘⇧P: Star note, Archive note, Move to folder…, History of this note and Open split view are now offered. Star it and look again: it says Unstar note.
5. ⌘⇧P `getting` offers **Open Getting started**, which opens the most recently changed note tagged `start` (here [[Palette start note]] or Welcome, which are both tagged `start`).
6. Click somewhere outside the editor (the Notes page) and press `?`: the shortcut sheet opens, grouped Global, Notes page, Editor, Vim, Tasks, Split view. Tab stays inside it, Esc closes it and puts you back where you were. Turn vim off (⌘⇧P `vim`) and the Vim section folds away.
7. On Dvorak (or any layout), the shortcuts follow the letters you type: ⌘P is wherever your P is.
8. ⌘⇧P `new folder` with the Folders section folded: it unfolds and the name field appears.
