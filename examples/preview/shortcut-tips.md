title: Shortcut tips
---
1. Click the gear at the bottom right and close Settings, three times. After the third, a tip says "⌘, opens Settings." (Ctrl+, off a Mac). Its **Show all shortcuts** opens the shortcut sheet.
2. Click **Search notes** in the sidebar three times, pressing Esc each time: no second tip in this visit. Reload and click it once more: the ⌘K tip appears, since the earlier clicks still count.
3. Tab to a button and press Enter on it a few times: keyboard use never counts. On a phone or tablet, no tips appear.
4. Turn tips off in Settings (⌘,) → Keyboard → **Shortcut tips**. To see the tips again from the start, clear `commonink.shortcutTips` from this site's local storage.
