title: Quick capture
---
1. **Install it on a phone.** Open this Preview in Chrome on Android and choose *Add to Home screen* (or *Install app*) from the ⋮ menu. On an iPhone, use Safari's Share button, then *Add to Home Screen*. It opens on its own, without the browser around it, as Common Ink.
2. **Share into it.** In another app (Chrome, Photos, YouTube…), tap *Share* and pick Common Ink. The capture screen shows what came: a title, text, a link and any images, all editable. *Today's note* is picked. Press *Save*, and it's at the end of today's journal note under `## Captured`: the link as a link card, the images embedded. (Sharing into an app is Android's share sheet; an iPhone doesn't offer it to web apps.)
3. **Pick somewhere else.** Share again and choose *Inbox* (made if it isn't there), or *Another note* and type a name. The choice is remembered on that phone for next time.
4. **On a desktop,** there's no share sheet, but the same screen opens from an address: add `/capture?title=A%20link&url=https://example.com` to this Preview's address, in place of what comes after its domain. Change the text, pick where it goes, and Save. Chrome on a desktop also offers to install the app, from the icon at the end of the address bar.
5. **Signed out?** Sharing still works: you sign in first, then land on the capture screen with what you shared.
