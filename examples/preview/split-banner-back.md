title: Split banners and back to the page
---
1. Open [[Split right]], then [[Split left]], and press ⌘⌥\ (or pick **Split view** from ⋯ in the top bar) so Split right opens beside it.
2. Click into Split left and press ⌘⇧E (or ⋯ → **Archive**) to archive it. The "This note is archived" banner shows over the left pane only; Split right has none and its text isn't pushed down.
3. Click into Split right, then press **Unarchive** on the left pane's banner: Split left comes back and its banner goes. Archive Split right too: each pane carries its own banner. Unarchive both before going on.
4. Close the split. Reload the page on **Tasks** (or open the app straight to `/tasks`). Open the task in Split right with its "Go to note" arrow, then press back (the top bar's ←, ⌘[, or the browser's back): you're on Tasks again.
5. Do the same from **Calendar** and **Notes**: back from a note you opened there always returns to that page.
