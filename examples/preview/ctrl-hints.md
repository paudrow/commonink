title: Ctrl in hints off a Mac
---
Use Windows or Linux for steps 1 to 3. On a Mac, each hint should still show ⌘.

1. Open **History**. The line under the heading says "Ctrl-click to add or skip". Before, it said ⌘-click.
2. Open [[Ctrl hints]] and click into its task. Hover the ⚙ at the end of the line. The tooltip ends in (Ctrl+.).
3. Open Getting started and press **Show me** on the Guide card. The card says "Press Ctrl+Z to take it back".
4. In Getting started, the search line reads "Press ⌘K (Ctrl+K off a Mac)". Under Keys, [[Tips]] now says to read ⌘ as Ctrl off a Mac and to press `?` for every shortcut.
