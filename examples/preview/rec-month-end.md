title: Month-end repeats
---
1. In [[Month ends]], tick **Pay rent** (`rec:31st`) until the new one lands in a month without a 31st, such as November: it's due on that month's last day, not skipped.
2. **Submit the timesheet** repeats `rec:last-day-1`: its chip reads "Last day − 1". **Pay the credit card** is `rec:last-day-2`, two days before the last day.
3. On **Change the water filter** (`rec:30th`), click the repeat chip and choose **More options…**. A note says shorter months have no 30th. Click **Use the last day of the month instead**: the summary becomes "Every month on the last day". Save.
4. In the same form, open the **Day** list: after the 31st it counts back from the end, from "last day" to "last day − 6".
5. In the quick-add bar (⌘⇧. or Ctrl+Shift+.), type `Pay rent every month on the 31st`. A note under the chips offers the last day. Click it: the words become "on the last day of every month".
6. In the quick-add bar, try `Timesheet the day before the last day of every month` and `Card 2 days before the end of the month`.
7. In a note, type `- [ ] Rent rec:31st`. The suggestions keep `31st` first (Enter changes nothing) and offer **Last day of the month** below it.
