title: Labels
---
1. Open [[Proposal]], then ⌘K (⌘⇧P) and choose **Labels of this note** (or press **History of this note** in the top bar). History opens on the note with its three labels as pins among its changes, newest first: **Agreed**, **Sent to Alex** and **v1**.
2. Pick **Agreed**. History compares it with the note now: the rewrite after it dropped two scope items, the timeline and the payment terms.
3. Change **Compare with** to **“v1”** to see everything that changed between the first draft and the agreed version.
4. Press **Restore to this version**. The note goes back to **Agreed** as one change, which you can undo from the toast. The pin now says **now**.
5. Rename a label with the pencil (say, "Agreed with Alex"), and delete **v1** with the bin. Only the name goes: the note and its History stay as they were.
6. **Label this version…** at the top of the note's History names the note as it is now; so does ⌘K "Label this version…" from the note, or `:label v4` with Vim keys on. On any change in History, **Label this version…** names the version right after it.
7. The **People** and **AI** filters in History still work, and the pins stay among the changes.
8. Delete the note, then open **Trash** and choose **Delete forever**: the dialog says its labels go too.
