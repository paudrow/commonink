title: Make and move events on the Calendar
---
1. Open **Calendar** in Week (`w`). Drag down tomorrow's column from 2 PM to 3:30 PM: the form opens on that time. Call it **Planning poker**, keep **Common Ink** as the calendar, tick **Also make a meeting note**, and save. The event shows, and the toast opens its note.
2. Drag **Planning poker** to another day and time, then drag its bottom edge to make it longer. Each change has an **Undo** (or ⌘Z).
3. With the keyboard: select an event and move it with the keys on the shortcut sheet (`?`, under Calendar).
4. Press `c` (or **New event**) for the form on its own: make an all-day event, and add a member of the workspace as an attendee.
5. Try dragging a **Standup** from **Launch team (demo)**: it's a subscribed feed, so it can't be moved here, and the app says so.
6. For Google: in **Calendars**, connect the stand-in with editing allowed and show **Dev (demo Google)**. Make an event in it, move one **Product sync** by dragging, and refresh: the moved one holds, and the others in the series stay put.
