title: Inks you earn
---
This Preview's sample notes already meet some goals, so a few inks may unlock at once in one toast. For a clean start, use the workspace menu at the top of the sidebar → **New team workspace…**, and clear `commonink.inks` from local storage.

1. Open Settings (⌘,) → Appearance → **Ink**. Indigo is chosen; locked inks are faded with a lock and say what earns them, with progress ("4 of 10"). Arrow keys move between them. Check light and dark.
2. Pick an ink you've earned with **Use it** on its toast, or in Settings. Links, highlights and buttons take its color. Reload: it draws in that ink straight away, with no flash of indigo.
3. Make notes until you have 10: "New ink: Viridian" appears. Connect an agent and have it edit a note for Cobalt; finish Getting started for Sepia.
