title: Hover text for the connection and save dots
---
1. Hover the small dot at the bottom right of the status bar, next to the theme button. It now says what it means: green is "Connected", so changes from agents, other tabs and collaborators show up live.
2. Turn off your network for a few seconds (or stop the dev server). The dot turns grey and its hover text says it is reconnecting and that your edits are kept and saved once the connection is back.
3. Type in a note and hover "Saved" at the top right, the other small dot. Its hover text now says what each state means: Edited, Saving…, Saved, and Not saved.
