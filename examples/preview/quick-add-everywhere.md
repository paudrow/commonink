title: Quick-add everywhere
---
1. On **Tasks**, click the words of "Return the library books" and add ` tomorrow`: it lights up as in the quick-add bar and the chip under it shows the date. Press Enter and it's the task's due date. Esc instead leaves the words as they were.
2. In [[Errands]], click at the end of "Call the bank" and type ` every week`: it gets a dotted underline and nothing else changes. Press Tab and it becomes `rec:weekly`. Undo takes it back in one step. "next tue" and "every 3 days" on the other lines are underlined when the cursor is on their line; click one to turn just that phrase.
3. On the board in [[Errands]], click **Add card** and type `Get stamps next fri`: the same highlight, chips and `#`/`@` suggestions as the quick-add bar. Enter adds it with `due:`. Click a card to edit it the same way.
4. Press ⌘⇧. (Ctrl+Shift+. off a Mac) on any page (Notes, Tasks, Tags, History) or in either pane of a split: the bar opens. From a note, Tab sends the task to the note in the pane you're in. It's the key that types ".", so on Dvorak it's ⌘⇧ with the E key; the split view keys (⌘⌥\, ⌘⌥[ and ⌘⌥]) follow your layout the same way.
5. With Vim on (the default; the status bar toggles it), the inline edit and the card fields use Vim too. Esc goes to normal mode, Esc again leaves, and Enter saves from either.
6. On **Tasks**, the Today card's sun sits on the "Today" line, lined up with Journal and Overdue below it.
