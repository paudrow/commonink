title: Whole note titles on phones
---
1. Open this Preview on a phone, or make the window narrower than 760px. Go to **Notes**.
2. Each card shows its whole title. [[A note with a long title that used to end after eight letters on a phone]] wraps onto a second line. Before, titles ended after about eight letters ("Getti…").
3. Tap a card. Its buttons (star, edit, archive, delete, expand) show on a row under the title. The other cards don't show them or keep room for them.
4. Open any note. Delete note is only in the ⋯ menu, with History, Move, Archive and Focus mode.
