title: Split view and the info panel have their own names
---
1. Open [[Tips]]. Hover the panel button at the top bar's right end: it says **Toggle info panel (⌘\)**, and its icon is a window with a column of lines. Press ⌘\ to hide and show the outline, backlinks and activity.
2. Open ⋯: the split item says **Open split view**, and its icon is two panes side by side. Pick it and choose a note.
3. Hover the lit split button in the top bar: it says **Close split view**. Hover the ✕ on either pane's bar: **Close split view, keep the other note**.
4. Press ⌘⇧P and type "split": the command is **Close split view** while split, **Open split view** otherwise. Press ? outside the editor: the Split view section says "in split view" throughout.
5. Hover a `[[link]]` in a note, a task's split button or a board's link card: each says **Open in split view**. Drag a link to the right edge: the drop zone says the same.
