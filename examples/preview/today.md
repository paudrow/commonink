title: Today, at the top of Tasks
---
1. Open **Tasks**: Today sits at the top, with today's journal note, then what's overdue, due today and starting today from [[Today's work]]. Those tasks aren't listed again below.
2. Click **Start today's note** (or **Open today's note** if a quick-add already made it): new days start from [[Daily note]] in `Templates/`, with `{{date}}` filled in. Edit it to change what a new day looks like.
3. Tick **Standup** in Today: it's done, and the next weekday's appears in its note.
4. Click a tag on a task below to filter Tasks: Today steps aside while a filter is on.
5. [[My day]] shows the same thing as a `::today` widget.
