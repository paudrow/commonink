title: Today
---
1. Open **Today** in the sidebar: today's journal note, then what's overdue, due today and starting today from [[Today's work]].
2. Click **Start today's journal** (or **Open today's journal** if a quick-add already made it): new days start from [[Journal]] in `Templates/`, with `{{date}}` filled in. Edit it to change what a new day looks like.
3. Tick **Standup** in Today: it's done, and the next weekday's appears in its note.
4. Click a tag on a task: Tasks opens, filtered to it.
5. [[My day]] shows the same thing as a `::today` widget.
