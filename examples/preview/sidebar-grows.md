title: A sidebar that grows with you
---
This Preview's sample notes already have contacts, calendars and files, so Contacts, Calendar and Assets show here. To see what a newcomer sees, open the workspace menu at the top of the sidebar and choose **New team workspace…**.

1. In the new workspace, the sidebar shows Notes, Tasks, Assets (the welcome notes include an image) and History, then Favorites, Folders and Tags. There's no Contacts, Calendar or Smart folders.
2. Press ⌘K and run **Go to Contacts**: Contacts appears in the sidebar, highlighted. Go back to Notes and it goes. Add a contact (New contact on the Contacts page) and it stays for good.
3. Run **New smart folder** in ⌘⇧P: the Smart folders section appears with the form under its +. Save one and the section stays.
4. Make an event on the Calendar page (⌘K, Go to Calendar, then New event): Calendar stays in the sidebar.
5. Open Settings (⌘,) → **Sidebar** and turn on **Always show Calendar** in a workspace without one: it shows, and stays after a reload in this browser.
