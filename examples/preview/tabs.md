title: Tabs
---
1. Open [[Tab one]]: it's a tab in the top bar, and so is Notes when you go there. Follow its link to Tab two: the tab you're on changes, and ← comes back.
2. ⌘-click (Ctrl-click) or middle-click [[Tab two]], a Notes card or a starred note: a new tab. Right-click any of them for Open in new tab. ⌘-click Today or a folder in the sidebar: a page in a tab.
3. Press ⌘T (⌘⌥T in a browser tab): quick open, into a new tab. ⌘W (⌘⌥W) closes a tab, ⌘⇧T (⌘⌥⇧T) brings it back with its back and forward. ⌃Tab, ⌘1…⌘9 switch.
4. Drag a tab along the strip to reorder it. Drag one to the right edge of the note: the outline shows the split, and it moves there. Drag a tab from one pane's strip to the other's, or onto the other note.
5. Drag a Notes card, a starred note, a [[Tab one]] link or a page from the sidebar onto a strip: it opens in a new tab where the line shows.
6. Drag a note's tab onto a folder in the sidebar: the note moves there.
7. Right-click a tab: Close others, Close to the right, Pin. A pinned tab stays first; open a link from it and it opens beside it.
8. Tab to the strip: ← → move, Enter shows, Delete closes, ⌘⇧PageUp/PageDown reorder, Shift+F10 the menu.
9. Reload: the tabs, which one you were on and each tab's back and forward are still there.
10. With vim on: `:tabnew Tab one`, `gt`, `gT`, `:tabonly`, `:q`.
