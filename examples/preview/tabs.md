title: Tabs
---
1. Open [[Tab one]]. Press ⌘K, type "Tab two" and press ⌥↵ (Alt+Enter): it opens in a new tab, and the tabs show in the top bar.
2. Middle-click a starred note in the sidebar, or a card on Notes: another tab. Or drag a Notes card onto the top bar.
3. Click a tab to switch. Following a link changes the tab you're on; it doesn't add one. Close a tab with its × or a middle-click.
4. Go to Notes, then click a tab: you're back in that note. Reload: your tabs are still there.
5. Split the window (⌘⌥\): each pane's bar has its own tabs, and ⌘⌥T opens a new tab in the focused pane.
6. With vim on: `:tabnew Tab one`, `gt` and `gT` to step through them, `:tabclose`.
