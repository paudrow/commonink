# Phone tour

Open this on a phone, or narrow the window below 760px. Everything here is one or two taps away.

## Getting around

The ☰ button opens the sidebar as a drawer. Tap outside it, press Esc, or pick a page or a note to close it.

## In a note

The top bar keeps the note's name, its star, Search and ⋯ More. More holds History, Move to folder, Archive, Focus mode and the side panel.
