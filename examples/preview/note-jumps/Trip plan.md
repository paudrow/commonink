# Trip plan

Four days in Lisbon. Start here: the flights are in [[Flights]], and where we're staying is in [[Lodging]].

## Day 1

- Land, drop the bags, walk to the river.
- Dinner somewhere in Alfama.
- Early night: the flight is long.

## Day 2

- Tram 28 before the crowds.
- Castle in the morning, the viewpoints after lunch.
- Pastries in Belém, the tower if there's time.
- Evening: the fado place the hosts recommend.

## Day 3

- Day trip to Sintra. Trains leave every 20 minutes from Rossio.
- Pena Palace first, then the Moorish castle on the walk down.
- Back by 6 for dinner.
- Put the cursor on this line, follow a link, then come back: you land here again.

## Day 4

- Market in the morning.
- Pack, then the [[Airport transfer]].

## Packing

- Walking shoes (the hills are steep).
- A light jacket for the evenings.
- Adapter: Portugal uses type F plugs.
- Copies of the bookings, in [[Lodging]] and [[Flights]].

## Budget

- Flights: booked.
- Lodging: booked, paid on arrival.
- Food: about 50 a day.
- Trains and trams: a Viva Viagem card each.
