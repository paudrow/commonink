title: Folders and Tags share one section
---
The sidebar's last section shows your folders or your tags, one at a time.

1. Look at the bottom of the sidebar: one header reads **Folders · Tags**, with Tags underlined.
2. Click **Folders**. The folder tree replaces the tags, and the + now makes a folder.
3. Click **Tags** again. The + makes a tag, and the sliders button opens rename and merge.
4. With Tags showing, drag a note from Notes over the **Folders** tab. The folders appear; drop the note on one to move it.
5. Reload. The tab you left on is still showing.
