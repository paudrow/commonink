A page that lists what may need tending, with a fix beside each one. The demo notes give it something to find.

1. Press ⌘⇧P (Ctrl+Shift+P off a Mac), type "check up" and pick **Check up on this workspace**. The Check-up page opens.
2. **Dead links** lists the link to "Q3 plan that was never written" in [[Check-up demo]]. Click **Open**: the note opens at that line.
3. Back on Check-up, **Duplicate contacts** shows Sam Ortiz and Samuel Ortiz (same email). Click **Go to Contacts**: its banner offers to merge them.
4. **Empty notes** lists [[Empty page]]. Click **Delete**. It goes to Trash, the toast has Undo, and the section goes away.
5. **Notes nothing links to** lists Loose idea, at the top level with no links, tag or star. Click **Archive** if you like; it's only a suggestion.
6. **Long-overdue tasks** lists "Send the survey results", due 45 days ago. "Water the plants" isn't there, because it repeats. Click **Open** to go to the task.
7. In a terminal, `commonink checkup get` prints the same list, and `--json` gives it as data.
