The side panel now finds notes that write this note's name without linking to it.

1. Open [[Garden plan]] with the side panel showing (⌘\ if it's hidden). Under Backlinks there's a folded **Unlinked mentions 2**.
2. Click it. Two rows from [[Saturday]]: "the garden plan" (its title) and "the veggie beds" (an alias in its frontmatter). The `garden plan` in code isn't listed.
3. Click **Link** on the first row. A toast says "Linked “garden plan” in Saturday". The row moves up to Backlinks, and Saturday now reads `[[Garden plan|garden plan]]`.
4. Click **Undo** in the toast. Saturday goes back to plain text and the row is back under Unlinked mentions.
5. Click a row (not its Link button) to open Saturday at that line.
