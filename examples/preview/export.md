title: Export and print
---
1. Open [[Export sampler]] and press **Share** in the top bar (the box with an arrow), or ⌘⇧S (Ctrl+Shift+S off a Mac). The menu has Copy link, Print…, Export as Markdown, Web page, Word or PDF.
2. **Copy link**, then paste it in a new tab: the same note opens.
3. **Print…**: a small dialog asks about the note's properties and collapsed sections. Press **Print**. The preview shows only the note, on white, with its title and today's date at the top and page numbers at the bottom. The tasks have their chips, and there are the `::tasks` list, the board, highlighted code, drawn math and the diagram. The embedded section of [[Export notes]] is drawn in place, the video is a card, and the alerts, footnote and collapsed sections print open.
4. Switch the app to dark (the moon in the status bar) and print again: the paper is still light.
5. Print again with **Include the note's properties** and **Keep collapsed sections closed** ticked: the properties table is at the top, and the collapsed alert and section stay shut.
6. **Share → PDF**: the same, with a note to choose **Save as PDF** in the print dialog.
7. **Share → Markdown** downloads `Export sampler.md`, exactly the note. **Share → Web page** downloads `Export sampler.html`: open it from your downloads. It shows the same note, the picture and diagram inside it and math drawn by the browser, with nothing to load.
8. ⌘K and type "export" or "print": each is a command there too. In Vim, `:share` opens the menu.
9. On a phone, Share is in **More** (⋯).
