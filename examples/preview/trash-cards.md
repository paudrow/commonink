title: Trash looks and works like Notes and Archive
---
Trash now lists the same cards as Notes and Archive, with the same filters, keys and bulk bar. Restore and Delete forever are its card and bulk actions.

1. In **Notes**, type "cooking" in the filter, press `x` on [[Pancakes]] and [[Soup stock]], and press the Delete key. Then delete [[Old meeting notes]] from its card's bin.
2. Click the **Trash** tab. Each note shows as a card with its title (not "Pancakes.md"), its text rendered (**local-first** is bold, the tasks are checkboxes), its tags, who deleted it and how many days are left.
3. The tag, folder and sort filters are still there. Click the **#cooking** chip on a card, or pick a tag from **Tag**: picking `cooking` also finds `#cooking/breakfast`. The sort menu offers **Recently deleted**, **Deleted longest ago** and **By title**.
4. Use the keys in the footer: `j`/`k` to move, `x` to select, `r` to restore, ⌫ to delete forever (it asks first), `/` to filter, Esc to clear the selection.
5. Select two cards with `x`: the bar offers **Restore** and **Delete forever** for both. Hover a card for the same two as icons.
6. **Empty trash** sits at the end of the filters. Online, only workspace owners see Delete forever and Empty trash.
7. Restore everything you deleted here, then type "cooking" on the Trash tab: the line under the list says "Also 2 in Notes."
8. Click between **Notes**, **Archive** and **Trash**, with Trash empty too: the search box, filters and the line under the tabs stay where they are, so nothing on the page jumps.
