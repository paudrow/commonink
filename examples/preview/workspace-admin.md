title: Workspaces, members and invites
---
This Preview has a team workspace, **Launch team**, that you own, with a second person in it: Sam Dev, an editor. Everything here is in **Workspace settings…** in the account menu at the bottom of the sidebar.

1. Open the account menu and switch to **Launch team**. Open the menu again and choose **Workspace settings…**. Sam is listed as an editor, with their email and when they joined.
2. Change Sam's role to **Viewer**. The toast says their connected agents were disconnected: an agent works with its person's role, so it connects again to pick up the new one. **Activity** at the bottom lists the change.
3. Under **Invite links**, the editor link shows who used it (Sam). The viewer link is still active: press **Revoke**, and it's gone. Pick a role and press **Copy a new link**: it's listed as active, expiring in 7 days.
4. Rename the workspace under **Name**. The account menu shows the new name.
5. Under **Leave**, the button is off: you're the only owner. Make Sam an **Owner** and it turns on. (Leaving takes you out of the team; the next deploy of this Preview makes a new one.)
6. Press **Remove** on Sam and confirm. Sam is out, and so are any agents Sam connected here.
7. Under **Delete workspace**, the button stays off until you type the workspace's exact name. Delete it: its notes, files, members and links go, and you land in your own workspace.
8. In your own workspace, **Workspace settings…** has **Name** but no **Leave** or **Delete**. Your own workspace is yours to keep.
