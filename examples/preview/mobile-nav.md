title: Phone layout
---
1. Open the Preview on a phone, or narrow the window below 760px. Tap ☰: the sidebar slides in over a dimmed page. Tap the dimmed page, press Esc, or pick Tasks: it closes. No page scrolls sideways.
2. On Notes, tap the round + at the bottom right to make a note. Tap the search icon in the top bar and open [[Phone tour]].
3. In [[Phone tour]], tap ⋯: History, Move to folder, Archive, Focus mode and the side panel are there. Pick Move to folder and move the note.
4. With a keyboard: Tab to ⋯, press Enter, move with the arrow keys, and press Esc. In the drawer, Tab stays inside it until you close it.
5. On a phone, the status bar has no `vim` chip, and vim starts off. On a desktop, both are as before.
6. Split a note on a wide window (⌘⌥\), then narrow it below 760px: only the focused pane shows, and its × brings back the other.
