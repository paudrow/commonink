title: A quieter top bar
---
1. Open [[Tips]]. The top bar shows the save status, Share, Star, **⋯**, Focus mode and Info panel. History, Move, Archive, Open split view and Delete moved into ⋯.
2. Click **⋯**: each item has its icon, Archive and Open split view show their keys (⌘⇧E, ⌘⌥\), and Move says where the note is ("At the top level"). Move with ↑ and ↓, then Esc: the focus goes back to ⋯.
3. Pick **Open split view** from ⋯ and choose a note. A lit split button comes back into the bar, and Split leaves the menu. Click it, or press ⌘⌥\, to close the split.
4. Press ⌘⇧E to archive the note, then Undo. Archive it from ⋯ instead: while it's archived the item reads **Unarchive note**.
5. Click **Share**: the menu ends at PDF, with no greyed-out Google Drive item.
6. Go to Tasks: there's no ⋯ with no note open. At a phone's width, ⋯ still holds the whole bar, as before.
