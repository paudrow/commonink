title: Correctness fixes from the UX review
---

1. Open [[Long read]], scroll to the bottom, press ⌘K (Ctrl+K off a Mac) and open [[Second long read]]. It opens at its title. Open it to the side with ⌘↵ instead, and the side pane starts at the title too.
2. Open Notes and find the card for [[Links in code]]. The `[[Note name]]`, `![[Note name]]` and `![alt](picture.png)` in code show as typed, and so do the lines in the code blocks. The link to Long read at the top still works.
3. In [[Scratch]], type `/tas`. The first pick is "Task list" (open tasks from across your notes) and the next is "Checkbox" (`- [ ]`). `/todo` puts Checkbox first, and `/time` puts "Current time" above "Timer".
4. Still in [[Scratch]], type three new lines, wait for the save, then delete two of them and wait again. Activity's entry for the note shows the net change (for example "+1 −0 · 2 saves"), and History shows the same numbers in its list and above the diff.
5. Rename [[Rename me]] by changing its heading and clicking below it. Activity says "You renamed …". History's entry for it says "Renamed" too.
6. Star [[Star then archive]], then archive it. It stays in Favorites, dimmed and marked Archived, and it opens from there.
