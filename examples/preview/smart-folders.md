title: Smart folders
---
1. Click the **+** on the Smart folders header. Name it "Meetings", pick Tag `meeting`, and Save: it opens in Notes and shows how many notes match ([[Standup]], [[Design review]], [[Retro]]).
2. In **Notes**, pick Tag `meeting` and Sort **By title**, then press **Save as smart folder**.
3. Hover a smart folder and click its sliders: rename it, change what it matches, tick "Just me", or delete it.
