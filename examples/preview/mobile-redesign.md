title: Phones

1. Open the Preview on a phone (or narrow the window below 760px with touch emulation on). A bar along the bottom has Today, Notes, Tasks, Search and Menu. Tap each: the page you're on is marked, Search takes the whole screen with a Cancel, and Menu opens the sidebar as a drawer that scrolls as one list.
2. Open [[Pocket notes]] and tap ⋯: the note's actions rise from the bottom. Pick Move to another folder: the folders come up the same way.
3. Tap into the note. A toolbar stands above the keyboard where the bottom bar was. On an empty line tap the checkbox, then type: it's a task. Tap `[[` and pick a note; tap `@` and pick a person; tap ✓ to hide the keyboard.
4. Open [[Trip board]]: a column nearly fills the screen, and the board swipes to the next one. Add card and a card's buttons are easy to hit.
5. Tap Tasks. A task's checkbox, its fields (the sliders) and Go to note are each 44px. Tap the sliders: Priority, Due and the rest come up from the bottom.
6. From Menu, open Contacts and tap a person: their name, then Edit note and Merge across the page. Open Calendar: Agenda first; Month shows dots, and a tap anywhere on a day opens it.
7. Rotate the phone, and try a notched one: nothing sits under the notch or the home bar, and no page scrolls sideways.
8. On a computer at 1280px or wider, nothing has changed, except that Tags, Contacts and Shared with me now scroll when they're taller than the window.
