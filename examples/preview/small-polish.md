title: A shorter quick-add hint, and a calmer empty History
---
Two small things that got in the way once you know the app.

1. Open Tasks. Under the "Add a task…" bar, the hint still gives an example ("Try 'Pay rent every month on the 1st #home'…"), says Enter adds, and shows the shortcut.
2. Add three tasks with the bar: type one, press Enter, and do it twice more.
3. Click in the bar and out again, or reload. The hint is now just "Ctrl+Shift+. opens this anywhere" (⌘⇧. on a Mac). It stays short in this browser from now on.
4. To see History empty, open a fresh vault (or a new workspace) and go to History. It says "Edit a note and its changes show up here…" with no "New note" button.
