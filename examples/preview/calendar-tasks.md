title: Calendar tasks and widget calendars
---
1. Open **Calendar**: [[Errands]] has tasks due today and tomorrow. Tick **Pick up the dry cleaning** from its checkbox. It stays on its day, struck through, and the toast has an **Undo**.
2. Tick **Water the plants**: it repeats every 3 days, so the next one appears 3 days on. Open [[Errands]] to see both lines.
3. With the keyboard: in Agenda (`a`), move to a task with the arrow keys and press `x` to tick it, `x` again to reopen it.
4. Open [[Just the team]] and press the gear on its `::agenda`: **Calendars** lists every calendar, all checked. Uncheck **US holidays** and save: the widget shows only the team's events, and its line says `calendars="…"`.
