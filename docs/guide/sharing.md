# Sharing and teams

## Workspaces

Everything lives in a workspace. You start with a personal one, and you can make team workspaces and switch between them from the account menu. Each person in a workspace has a role:

| Role | Can |
| --- | --- |
| Owner | Everything, plus members, invites, settings and deleting the workspace |
| Editor | Read and change every note, file, task and calendar |
| Viewer | Read; their agents only get the read tools |

**Workspace settings…** in the account menu manages people. Owners make invite links for editors or viewers (each works once, for 7 days), change roles and remove people. Removing someone also disconnects their agents there. **Activity** lists every change to the workspace's settings.

## Share a note

Share a note or a folder with people outside the workspace: the **Share** button in a note's top bar (⌘⇧S), then **Share…**. Share it by email, or with anyone who has the link, as a viewer or an editor, and optionally until a date. People without an account get it when they sign in with that email.

Someone you share a note with sees that note and nothing else: not search, tasks or other notes. A share follows the note through renames and moves.

## Print and export

From **Share**: print a note, or export it as Markdown, a web page, Word or PDF. ⌘⇧P, then **Export all notes (.zip)**, downloads every note and file in the workspace as markdown, folders kept, ready to open in Obsidian or any editor. A folder's menu exports just that folder.

## Get things back

- **Undo.** Anything you can undo says so in a toast: archiving, moving, ticking a task, an agent's edit. ⌘Z presses the newest Undo when you aren't typing.
- **History.** Every change to every note is kept with who made it. Compare any two versions and restore one, as one change you can undo too.
- **Labels.** Label a version ("v1", "Sent to Alex") to come back to it.
- **Trash.** Deleted notes, files and folders wait 30 days, and come back where they were, links included.
